Privacy Policy — Quizistan Academy

Effective date: October 2026  ·  App: Quizistan Academy ("Board ki Tayyari")

Quizistan Academy is an exam-preparation app for Punjab board students (classes 9–12). This policy explains what data the app collects and how it is used.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: when you sign in (e.g. via Google Sign-In), we receive your name, email address and profile photo through Firebase Authentication.
  • Learning activity: quiz attempts, scores, progress and preferences are stored in Firebase Firestore so your progress syncs across your devices.
  • Advertising identifiers: the free version of the app shows ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device and advertising identifiers to serve and measure ads.
  • Purchase information: premium subscriptions are processed by Google Play Billing; we receive confirmation of your purchase status but not your payment details.
  • Diagnostics: crash reports and basic performance data may be collected via Firebase services to keep the app stable.

2. How we use information

  • To create and manage your account and sync your study progress.
  • To show relevant ads in the free version (ads are removed for premium users).
  • To unlock premium features after purchase.
  • To diagnose crashes and improve the app.

We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

3. Third-party services

The app uses the following third-party services, each with its own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase (authentication, database, diagnostics)
  • Google AdMob (advertising)
  • Google Play Billing (in-app purchases)

4. Children's privacy

Quizistan Academy is used by students, some of whom may be under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

5. Data retention and security

Your account data is kept for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us. We use industry-standard safeguards, but no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

6. Your choices

  • You may use the app without creating an account, with limited features.
  • You can reset your advertising identifier in your device settings to limit ad personalization.
  • You can request a copy or deletion of your data by contacting us.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the effective date updated above.
