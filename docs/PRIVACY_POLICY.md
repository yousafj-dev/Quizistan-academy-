# Privacy Policy — Quizistan Academy

**Last updated:** October 8, 2026

Quizistan Academy ("we", "our", "the app") is an exam-preparation app for Punjab board students (classes 9–12). This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

## 1. Data We Collect

**Account data:** When you sign in, we store your name and email address via Firebase Authentication (Google).

**Learning data:** Your quiz attempts, scores, saved questions, and tests you create are stored in Firebase Firestore so your progress is available across your devices.

**Device data:** We may collect basic device information (device model, OS version, app version) for crash reporting and improving the app.

## 2. How We Use Your Data

- To run the app's features: quizzes, question bank, test maker, live test rooms, and progress tracking.
- To fix bugs and improve performance.
- We **do not sell** your personal data to anyone.

## 3. Advertisements

The free version of the app shows ads served by **Google AdMob**. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to show relevant ads, as described in [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [AdMob's data practices](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543).

## 4. In-App Purchases

Premium subscriptions are processed securely by **Google Play Billing**. We do not see or store your payment card details — payments are handled entirely by Google.

## 5. Data Sharing

We share data only with the service providers needed to run the app:
- **Google Firebase** (authentication, database, crash reporting)
- **Google AdMob** (advertising in the free plan)
- **Google Play** (in-app purchases)

We do not share your data with any other third party.

## 6. Data Security

Your data is stored on Google's secure Firebase servers with industry-standard encryption. While we take reasonable steps to protect it, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

## 7. Children's Privacy

Quizistan Academy is designed for school students, including users under 18. We encourage parents and guardians to supervise their children's use of the app. We do not knowingly collect more data from children than needed to run the app's features. If you are a parent and believe your child has provided personal data beyond what this policy describes, contact us and we will delete it.

## 8. Your Rights

You may request a copy of your data or ask us to delete your account and associated data at any time by contacting us at the email below. Deleting your account removes your profile, quiz history, and saved questions from our servers.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above and notify users inside the app where appropriate.
